# TRACK-A-06 Contract Result

TRACK-A-06 adds the SourcePage view model contract as governance-only work.

The bundle defines:

- canonical SourcePageView schema
- SourcePageView publication policy inventory
- public-safe SourcePage examples for minimal, recorded fixture, placeholder,
  and gap-oriented pages
- stdlib-only validator and unittest coverage
- audit evidence for product-boundary no-goals

The bundle does not activate source-page runtime, source connectors, source
sync, live probes, downloads, uploads, accounts, telemetry, native clients,
hosted behavior, public routes, generated site artifacts, or master-index
mutation.
