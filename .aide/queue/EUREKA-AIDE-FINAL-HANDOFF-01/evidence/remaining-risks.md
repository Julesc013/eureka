# Remaining Risks

- AIDE Lite proves repo-local governance and packet readiness, not arbitrary
  product implementation quality.
- Exact provider tokenization, hidden reasoning token usage, cached-token
  discounts, and billing are not measured.
- Live provider/model/network routing remains disabled.
- Product implementation work must wait for staged queue review.
- Future upstream AIDE synchronization is still needed for target-specific
  golden tasks and selftest fallback learnings.
