## GOAL

docs, evals, architecture evidence, or AIDE operating metadata without changing

- Inspect `.aide/context/latest-context-packet.md`, repo maps, and the golden
  task evidence before selecting the specific maintenance task.

  architecture-maintenance evidence, or AIDE operating metadata.

- Do not change product behavior or product source files.
