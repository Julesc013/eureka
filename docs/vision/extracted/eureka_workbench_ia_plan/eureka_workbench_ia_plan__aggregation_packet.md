# Aggregation Packet — Eureka Workbench, IA Connector, and Production Path

## One-Paragraph Summary

This chat documents Eureka’s transition from broad track planning and agent-generated scaffolding into a Workbench-centered product doctrine built around a local source-backed discovery loop. Early work focused on commit discipline, prompt IDs, queue control, and massive track/prompt generation. The conversation then confronted the risk that H-series artifacts were mostly scaffolding rather than product runtime. Later visible branch status suggested HUNT, PLAY, and an IA metadata pilot had produced a real local vertical slice through source cache, evidence, review, and reviewed index proof. The final synthesis was that the Workbench should be the internal/operator superset of the final product, and the IA connector should be progressive and reviewed, feeding visible result lanes and Hunts before SYN, DOMAIN, SCOUT, F, broader sources, public hosting, or marketplace features.

## Main Themes

- Scaffolding is useful but not product.
- Review before truth governs sources, AI, extraction, and public records.
- Internet Archive should be built end-to-end progressively, not crawled synchronously.
- Workbench should be Eureka Mission Control and internal superset.
- Public web/native/mobile should be safe projections over shared packets.
- SYN should test visible product behavior.
- Public production remains far away and must not be claimed prematurely.
