# Seed Batch Review Packet

Seed batch review packets group candidates and SCOUT paths for operator review.
They may produce promotion previews, local apply handoffs, and snapshot refresh
handoffs, but those outputs are not promotion or public index mutation.

Public projections may show read-only summaries. Public users cannot accept,
reject, promote, mutate, download, extract, or execute source actions.

Operator review remains policy-gated and separate from local apply.
