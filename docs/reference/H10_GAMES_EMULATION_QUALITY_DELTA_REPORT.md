# H10 Games/Emulation Quality Delta Report

H10 review integration is a wave-level rehearsal. It consumes committed
fixture replay outputs plus blocked or dry metadata-only live-probe
reports, then produces review seeds, quality delta, connector scorecard
previews, source-pack previews, postmortem evidence, and next-phase
recommendations.

It is not promotion. Game/software identity, platform/release/edition,
emulator compatibility, preservation hash-set, ROM/disc/media identity,
game relation, emulator/action, and rights/safety records remain
candidates only. They are not accepted truth, legal acquisition proof,
ROM or disc authenticity proof, emulator compatibility proof,
playability, installability, rights clearance, malware safety, content
safety, privacy safety, production quality, or production coverage.

Fixture, live, and blocked outputs are handled as local evidence. If
operator approval is missing, blocked live-probe reports are recorded
without inventing live evidence. Review seeds are not review decisions.
Source-cache and evidence outputs are previews only.

H10 routes to H11 when the wave is coherent because storefront and
app-store policy packs are the next metadata-source family. J1 risky
actions, K semantic/AI, and L wider clients remain deferred unless their
gates are explicitly opened.

No-goals: live calls, catalog/API/software-list/hash-set fetches,
downloads, uploads, hash submissions, emulator/game/install execution,
acquisition actions, scraping, crawling, bypass, restricted-source
access, source sync, public/master index mutation, product behavior
change, and truth acceptance.

Validation commands include:

- `python scripts/validate_h10_games_emulation_review_quality_audit.py`
- `python scripts/integrate_h10_games_emulation_review.py --input-dir examples/connectors/h10_games_emulation/replay_results --check`
- `python scripts/summarize_h10_games_emulation_quality_delta.py --input-dir examples/connectors/h10_games_emulation/review_integration --check`
- `python scripts/audit_h10_games_emulation_wave.py --check`
