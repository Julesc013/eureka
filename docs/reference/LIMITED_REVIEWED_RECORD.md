# Limited Reviewed Record

Limited reviewed records carry evidence references, candidate references, review
batch references, limitations, prohibited claims, and explicit false claim
flags.

They are useful for corpus growth and internal review because they preserve
what is known without overstating what has been verified.

They are not:

- verified downloadable artifacts
- malware-clean files
- rights-cleared records
- compatibility-guaranteed drivers or software
- OCR-verified text
- scan-complete documents
