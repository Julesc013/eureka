# G0 Score Signal

A score signal is one deterministic contribution to a fixture score. Required
families include query/title fit, identifiers, aliases, domain fit, platform
fit, source-trust prior, metadata completeness, provenance strength, F0 member
manifest relevance, SCOUT relation strength, DOMAIN promote/suppress rules,
rights/safety penalties, duplicate and near-miss penalties, user-cost penalty,
and blocked-action penalty.

Every signal must provide an explanation, allowed inputs, review posture,
uncertainty through the containing score breakdown, and a non-claim boundary.
Signals do not create evidence or truth.
