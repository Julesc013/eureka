# Public Search Status Badges

Status badges are text labels, not color-only indicators.

Required badges include:

- Verified
- Reviewed metadata
- Reviewed source lead
- Candidate
- Known need
- Absence
- Near miss
- review required
- limited claim
- no download
- no safety claim
- no rights claim

Badges communicate result posture and prevent candidates or limited source leads from being mistaken for verified artifacts.
