# Extraction Manifest Candidate Contract

`contracts/control_schemas/previews/extraction/extraction_manifest_candidate.v0.json` describes bounded Tier 2 manifest previews from recognized small manifest-like files such as `package.json`, `manifest.json`, `pyproject.toml`, `PKG-INFO`, and `METADATA`.

Manifest candidates are evidence previews only. They do not prove identity, rights, safety, compatibility, or installability.
