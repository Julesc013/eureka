- `schema_version`
- `section_id`
- `domain_id: manuals_docs_scans`
- `candidate_count`
- `candidate_refs`
- `download_performed: false`
- `file_fetch_performed: false`

- `rights_clearance_claim_created: false`

- `ocr_quality_claim_created: false`

Public search cards from this section must use candidate status. They are not
verified artifacts and do not expose download, OCR, or rights-clearance claims.
