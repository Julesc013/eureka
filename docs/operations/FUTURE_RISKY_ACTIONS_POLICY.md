# Future Risky Actions Policy

Download, mirror, install, execute, emulate, submit, and import actions are disabled in J0.

Future work must add policy gates before any such action can be implemented:

- rights review
- risk review
- malware/executable policy
- explicit user confirmation
- action audit trail
- rollback or incident policy where relevant
- no auto-run

Until those gates exist, J0 emits blocked action reports and safe alternatives only.
