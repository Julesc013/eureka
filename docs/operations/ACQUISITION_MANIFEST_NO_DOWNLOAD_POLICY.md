# Acquisition Manifest No Download Policy

J0 acquisition manifests are descriptive only.

They may describe source refs, candidate access paths, locator summaries, fixity refs, and reviews needed before future access. They must not open external URLs, fetch remote resources, download files, mirror files, install artifacts, execute files, emulate anything, or approve source access.

Any future acquisition behavior needs a separate reviewed policy with rights review, risk review, user confirmation, action audit, and no auto-run guarantees.
