# Local Track Completion Standard

Proof levels:

- L0: scaffold only.
- L1: command/runtime proof.
- L2: persistence/integration proof.
- L3: local workbench proof.
- L4: fresh-machine or LAN proof.
- L5: hosted/public proof.

LOCAL requires L1 through L4 proof before handing back to F0. L5 is not part of LOCAL and does not imply production or public launch readiness.

Future F, G, H, I, J, K, D, C, E, and L work must plug into Local Appliance capabilities when applicable instead of bypassing the local product loop.
