# Connector Scorecard Review

Connector scorecards should be reviewed as evidence about process, not truth
about source contents. A passing scorecard can support H1 planning, but it does
not approve live access, source sync, downloads, or future connectors.

Allowed metrics are limited to fixture replay, policy blocks, candidate counts,
review entry counts, quality/postmortem availability, warnings, and blockers.
Production readiness and external superiority claims are forbidden.
