# Citation And Export Policy

Citation bundles and export manifests preserve refs, posture, and limitations.

They do not:

- accept evidence or candidates
- import, submit, publish, or accept packs
- mutate public or master indexes
- claim rights clearance, malware safety, verified installability, or production readiness

Generated outputs may be written only to `examples/actions/`, `control/audits/**/generated/`, or explicit temporary test directories.
