# Native Read-Only Client Policy

Native clients remain read-only in C-BUNDLE-01.

They may read snapshot fixtures, relay fixture envelopes, action manifests, and public-safe view records. They must show source, evidence, rights, risk, action posture, limitations, and no-claims when present.

They must not call live sources, mutate stores, mutate public or master indexes, accept evidence or candidates, store credentials, enable accounts, upload data, or emit telemetry.
