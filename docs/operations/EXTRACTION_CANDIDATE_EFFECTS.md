# Extraction Candidate Effects

Extraction candidate effects translate member listings and manifest candidates into source-cache, evidence, and review seed previews.

Candidate effects are not accepted candidates. Evidence previews are not accepted evidence. Review seed previews are not review decisions. Public and master index mutation remain forbidden.

F-BUNDLE-02 routes those candidate effects into search-gap, review-seed, WorkUnit-seed, usefulness, and Track G handoff previews. This routing remains fixture-only and does not change public search behavior.
