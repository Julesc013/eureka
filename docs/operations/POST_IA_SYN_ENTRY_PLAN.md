proves a full local-source loop without public launch or extraction claims.

- IA-00 through IA-07 validators pass.
- The IA closeout validator passes.
- Hard blockers and warnings are zero.
- SYN consumes IA outputs as local proof material, not source truth.

- reviewed local IA record shape and non-claim language
- source/evidence/candidate/review/promotion boundary reports
- search/object/absence packet proof patterns

- extraction
- live source expansion
- model/provider calls
- deployment
- production readiness or public launch readiness claims
