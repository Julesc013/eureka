# IA Metadata Connector Postmortem

The IA connector postmortem records what the first connector pattern proved and
what must wait for H0.

Required sections:

- what worked
- what failed
- policy gaps
- data shape gaps
- source-cache mapping gaps
- evidence mapping gaps
- review gaps
- quality delta summary
- safety boundary assessment
- H0 implications
- H1 reuse recommendations
- next connector recommendation
- do-not-repeat risks

The postmortem does not approve future connectors automatically. It recommends
H0 source operating system work before broad H1 expansion.
