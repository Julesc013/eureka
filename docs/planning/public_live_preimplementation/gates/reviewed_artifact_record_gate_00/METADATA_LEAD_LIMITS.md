# Metadata Lead Limits

Metadata and support pages can support investigation. They cannot alone prove artifact identity, integrity, safety, rights, or acquisition posture.

Allowed public phrasing:

- metadata lead
- source lead
- reviewed support fact
- evidence needed
- near miss

Forbidden public phrasing for the current corpus:

- verified artifact
- reviewed artifact record
- safe download
- installable artifact
- rights cleared
- malware safe
