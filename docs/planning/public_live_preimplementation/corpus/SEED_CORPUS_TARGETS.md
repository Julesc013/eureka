# Seed Corpus Targets

## Alpha Target

- 50 hard queries evaluated.
- 200 reviewed records.
- 500 useful candidates, needs, near misses, or bounded absences.
- At least one public-safe evidence summary per reviewed record.
- Search usefulness scorecard completed.
