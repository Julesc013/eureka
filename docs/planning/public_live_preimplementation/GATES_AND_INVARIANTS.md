# Gates And Invariants

## Truth Gates

- Review events create promoted truth.
- Source observations, AI output, synthetic fixtures, and candidates cannot
  become reviewed records without review.
- Every reviewed public record must cite review evidence or absence rationale.

## Fallback Gates

- Fallback is a run mode, not a UI/source shortcut.
- Fallback must be source-allowlisted, budgeted, policy-gated, and disableable.
- Fallback may create `candidate`, `need`, `near_miss`, `policy_blocked`, or
  unavailable states, not `verified`.

## Surface Gates

- Renderers cannot query sources.
- Renderers cannot mutate records or promote candidates.
- Renderers cannot hide status, evidence posture, or blocked actions.

## Launch Gates

- Public scope and non-goals are explicit.
- No public writes, downloads, extraction, emulation, or public source fanout.
- Workbench remains private/operator-only.
- Hard-query usefulness evaluation passes the agreed threshold.
- Reviewed seed corpus and reviewed artifact record gate pass.
- Manual launch approval is recorded.
