# Snapshot Result Card Projection

Result-card projection translates snapshot records and candidates into public-search card states. Supported states include verified, reviewed metadata record, reviewed source lead, candidate, near miss, known need, absence, and source lead.

Cards are labels and summaries, not new evidence. Candidate-like cards must not be accepted truth. Limited reviewed metadata/source-lead cards must not become verified artifact cards.

Forbidden claims remain false on every card:

- verified download
- malware clean
- rights cleared
- scan complete
- OCR quality known
- compatibility guaranteed
