# Snapshot Reviewed Needs And Absences

Reviewed known needs are unresolved review tasks, not accepted object truth.
Reviewed bounded absences are scoped statements about missing evidence in the
reviewed fixture/projection set, not universal absence claims.

Public-search and no-results projections can show these states to help review
planning. They must not present them as resolved artifacts, downloadable files,
safety assertions, rights clearance, or launch readiness.
