# PUBLIC-ALPHA-REASSESS-06

`PUBLIC-ALPHA-REASSESS-06` reassesses the read-only public alpha after
`SNAPSHOT-REFRESH-06` projected review-batch apply results into snapshot,
relay, public-search, and public-alpha handoffs.

The reviewed corpus improved from 4 to 12 limited reviewed projections. That
is useful for internal demo and review, but it is not a launch signal. Limited
reviewed metadata/source-lead records are not verified artifacts, reviewed
known needs are not resolved objects, and bounded absences are not universal
absence claims.

The reassessment remains example-only and read-only. It does not deploy, write
`site/dist`, mutate public/master indexes, call live sources, fetch files, OCR
documents, extract artifacts, call model providers, or claim public readiness.

Launch remains deferred because the limited reviewed projection count is below
the 25-record threshold, reviewed artifact records do not exist through a
governed gate, indexless fallback is missing, search usefulness evaluation is
missing, external full discovery has not passed for the current stack, the stack
has not been promoted to `main`, and no public launch approval is active.
