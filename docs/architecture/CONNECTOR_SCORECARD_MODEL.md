# Connector Scorecard Model

Connector scorecards sit after the H0-BUNDLE-02 connector interface. They
summarize whether a connector family or concrete connector has fixture replay,
policy evaluation, a blocked or approved live-probe envelope, output envelopes,
review integration, quality delta, and coverage artifacts.

The scorecard does not execute connectors. It does not approve future source
families or claim production readiness. Its metric vocabulary is deliberately
small: fixture pass/fail counts, policy block counts, candidate counts, review
entry counts, warning counts, and blocker counts.
