# Review Batch Apply Eligibility

Eligibility is conservative and deterministic. A candidate must have existing
review-batch evidence, sufficient metadata, and a safe limited claim scope.

Eligible candidates can become limited metadata records or source leads only.
Software, driver, document, and media candidates remain non-artifact records
unless a future reviewed-artifact gate explicitly enables stronger claims.

Candidates not selected remain in the non-applied report with a reason. Low
confidence candidates, duplicated live metadata decisions, and candidates that
need further identity, rights, safety, OCR, scan, or compatibility work stay
review-required.

Known needs and bounded absences may be reviewed as unresolved needs or bounded
absence records. They are not object truth and are not universal absence claims.
