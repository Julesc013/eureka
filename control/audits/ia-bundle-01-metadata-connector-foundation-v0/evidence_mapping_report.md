# Evidence Mapping Report

Normalized IA metadata can produce evidence candidate previews for later review.
Preview kinds:

- title claim candidate
- mediatype claim candidate
- file-list/member claim candidate
- creator/date claim candidate
- source locator claim candidate
- collection membership claim candidate

These previews are not accepted evidence and do not mutate the evidence ledger.
They cannot prove downloaded file availability, rights clearance, malware
safety, verified installability, identity, public truth, or master-index
mutation.
