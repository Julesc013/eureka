# Warning Disposition

All warnings are disposed for promotion.

Classifications:

- `harmless_for_promotion`: resolved contract taxonomy warning, resolved generated artifact drift, and prior R0 warning records that do not block the recovered baseline.
- `deferred_with_expiry`: remaining legacy runtime leakage allowlist debt, expiring before any later production/public-launch readiness review.
- `child_task_created`: legacy leakage follow-up remains tracked as `R0-REMEDIATION-LEGACY-LEAKAGE-01`.

No warning blocks F0 and no warning blocks plan-only promotion readiness.
