# Examples Taxonomy Closeout v1

## Summary

`examples/` remains public-safe fixture and proof material. It is still sprawling,
but the sprawl is classified and future moves are constrained to durable concept
families.

## Decision

Use migration-map-first cleanup. Do not collapse connector fixtures or checksumed
examples in bulk.
